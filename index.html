Student Service Queue Management System

A practical demonstration of the Queue data structure and FIFO (First In, First Out).

How a queue works

A queue follows the First In, First Out (FIFO) principle. The first student added is the first student served. New students join at the rear, while service takes place at the front.

Queue operations demonstrated

Enqueue
Adds a student to the rear of the queue.
Dequeue
Removes and serves the student at the front.
Peek / Front
Displays the next student without removing them.
Size
Counts how many students are waiting.
isEmpty
Checks whether the queue has any students.

Why queues are necessary

A student service office may receive several students at the same time. A queue keeps requests in arrival order, makes the next person to serve clear, and reduces confusion. This prototype uses a JavaScript array to demonstrate FIFO behaviour. Its data is temporary and resets when the page is refreshed; a real system could use a database for permanent storage.
